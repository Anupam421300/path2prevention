Terms of Service

1. What Path2Prevention does
Path2Prevention helps you log lifestyle signals, view a personalised diabetes risk score, and receive evidence-based self-management guidance.

2. Educational use only
Path2Prevention is not a medical device, not a diagnostic service, and not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

3. Your inputs matter
Your risk score and recommendations depend on the accuracy of the information you provide, including lifestyle logs, body measurements, family history, and optional lab values.

4. Clinical safety
If your self-reported lab values fall into diabetes-range thresholds, Path2Prevention may surface a safety alert encouraging you to consult a qualified healthcare professional.

5. Account and data
You may delete your account, which permanently removes associated user data from the platform.
